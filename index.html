My Photography

5 year old me could have never guessed that I would get into photography. From a young age, I often hid from the camera or was not the happiest person when my mom would ask me to smile for "only a couple" of family photos. Some things never change and I still am not the happiest when we take our family photos. However, once I was behind a camera instead of in front of it, I started enjoying Photography a lot more. I remember originally seeing flashy photos on instagram with beautiful colors and that was what inspired me to begin taking photos. I didn't really know where to begin as I was just a kid in high school at the time with nothing but my iPhone camera. I started to take photos of things I would see every day and tried to color grade them in lightroom to make my photos seem super flashy and cool. While most were duds, some actually turned out to look kind of cool. I remember showing my track coach at the time and then he asked an important question. What was the story behind my photo? The one I showed him was simply of a car parked outside on the street with good colors in the image, but very basic. After his comment, I begun to look more in depth about the photos I admired online and then learned what set them aside from mine. Besides the years of technical experience and technology, their photos often created a certain vibe that could draw out some feeling or emotion, which my photos failed to do. I then learned that its important to have an emotion, feeling, or message in mind when taking and editing a photo, as that is truly how you can produce some very beautiful looking pictures.
